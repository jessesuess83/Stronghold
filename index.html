How to Play

Objective

Control 4 Castles at the end of your turn to win the game.

Your Turn

Choose one action.

Move a Knight

Move one Knight up to 5 intersections.

Knights:

  • may cross your own Walls.
  • may not cross enemy Walls.
  • may not move through enemy Knights.
  • must end on an empty intersection.

Build a Wall

Place one Wall on an empty edge.

A Wall must:

  • connect to one of your Knights or Walls.
  • not touch an enemy Knight or enemy Wall.

Destroy a Wall

A Knight may destroy one adjacent enemy Wall.

Destroyed Walls return to their owner’s supply.

Knights protect adjacent Walls. Protected Walls cannot be destroyed.

Build a Castle

Place a Castle on an empty hex if:

  • its six sides include 4 Walls of your color.
  • it is at least 1 hex away from every Capital and Castle.

Capturing Knights

A Knight is captured if it has no legal move because it is blocked by enemy Knights and/or Walls.

Captured Knights immediately return to the furthest empty intersection adjacent to their Capital.

Capturing Castles

If 4 Walls surrounding an enemy Castle become your Walls, capture that Castle by flipping it to your color.

Capitals

  • Capitals are permanent and cannot be captured.
  • Knights always return to their own Capital when captured.
